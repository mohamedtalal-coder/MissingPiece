Joinery & Materials

Handcrafted from solid quartersawn French White Oak. The frame is joined exclusively through precision mortise-and-tenon nodes without synthetic plastic dowels or metal brackets.

Care & Longevity

Wipe clean with a dry or lightly dampened microfiber cloth. Reapply natural beeswax or hardwax oil once every two years to feed the oak fibers. Protect from prolonged direct heat radiators.
