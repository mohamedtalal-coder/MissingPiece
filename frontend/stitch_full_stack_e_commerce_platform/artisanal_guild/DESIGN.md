## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, deliberate pacing, and tactile satisfaction of fine tabletop craft. Designed for collectors, hobbyists, and gift-givers who value slow living, physical materiality, and heirloom objects, the interface balances editorial luxury with the grounding atmosphere of an artisan's workshop.

### Design Aesthetic: Tactile Editorial Craft
The visual language merges warm, parchment-toned minimalism with physical, tactile metaphors. It deliberately rejects cold clinical grids, synthetic neons, and sterile tech aesthetics in favor of:
- **Materiality:** Soft paper and parchment surfaces layered like archival book stock and hand-cut puzzle board.
- **Craftsmanship Details:** Slender debossed lines, muted keyline borders reminiscent of box construction, and warm, diffused ambient lighting.
- **Pacing:** Generous whitespace, deliberate vertical cadence, and literary editorial framing that invite leisurely exploration rather than hurried checkout funnels.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet satisfaction, nostalgic warmth, tactile anticipation, and the mindful focus of slotting the final interlocking piece into place.

## Colors

The color system draws directly from raw natural materials: aged botanical paper, hand-rubbed cognac leather, kiln-fired terracotta, moss-grown cedar, and deep iron gall ink.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#C25E2E` - Terracotta Artisan):** Key actionable focal points, primary CTA buttons, highlight badges, and purchase paths.
- **Secondary (`#4A6B53` - Moss Sage):** Balanced organic secondary tone; indicates natural materials, difficulty ratings, collection badges, and sustainable craft certifications.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Cognac Amber):** Used sparingly for limited editions, mystery drops, metallic foil accents, and celebratory highlights.
- **Neutral (`#292524` - Slate Charcoal / Espresso):** Anchoring typography, deep borders, and structural framing. Replaces pure black to maintain an ink-on-paper warmth.

### Surface System (Parchment Layers)
- **Base Canvas (`#FDFBF7`):** Pure parchment canvas providing a soft, warm foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#F7F3EB`):** Cards, input backings, and drawer containers.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#EDE5D8`):** Recessed containers, hover states, filter rails, and segmented controls.
- **Border Subtle (`#E6DEC9`):** Fine architectural keylines separating artisan modules.
- **Text Muted (`#78716C`):** Secondary metadata, piece counts, dimension specs, and provenance notes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates tension between classical bookplate elegance and contemporary clarity.

### Family Pairings
- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the legacy of antique bookshops and collector folios. Used exclusively for storytelling, collection titles, product names, and chapter headings. It introduces emotional pause and prestige.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its friendly geometry, open counters, and high legibility at dense scales. Handles spec sheets, component interactions, piece specifications, and pricing without visual clutter.

### Typographic Rules
- Never use `Playfair Display` in all-caps for long strings; reserve all-caps styling for short `label-sm` category badges or edition numbers.
- Maintain comfortable measure for narrative text: restrict long-form puzzle lore and artist stories to a maximum line length of 65 characters (`ch`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a balanced fluid grid anchored by rhythmic 8pt increments, creating an organized, catalog-style balance.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `margin-lg` (4rem) outer margins and `gutter-lg` (2rem) columns. Max canvas width constrained to 1440px for comfortable scanning.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin-md` (2.5rem) margins and `gutter` (1.5rem) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4-column grid with `margin` (1.5rem) margins and `gutter-sm` (1rem) gutters.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Reserved for icon-to-label gaps, tag paddings, and piece meta-indicators.
- **Component Spacing (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Internal padding for puzzle collection cards, form groups, and filter chips.
- **Macro Layout (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`):** Inter-section breathing room separating artisanal narratives, product galleries, and maker profiles.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic multi-color drop shadows, depth is achieved through layered tactile surfaces paired with umber-tinted ambient occlusion. Elements feel resting upon, inset into, or gently elevated above a workbench.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Recessed / Inset (Well):** 
   - Surface: `#EDE5D8`
   - Shadow: `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.06)`
   - Used for search bars, quantity adjusters, and thumbnail trays.
2. **Resting (Base Cards & Tiles):**
   - Surface: `#F7F3EB`
   - Border: `1px solid #E6DEC9`
   - Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.04), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.03)`
3. **Floating / Hovered (Active Piece / Interactive Card):**
   - Surface: `#FDFBF7`
   - Border: `1px solid #D6CEB9`
   - Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(120, 53, 15, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.04)`
   - Subtle vertical translation (`translateY(-2px)`) to mimic lifting a piece from the board.
4. **Modal & Drawer Overlays:**
   - Backdrop: `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.45)` with a delicate `blur(4px)` backdrop filter.
   - Surface: `#FDFBF7`
   - Shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.22)`

## Shapes

The geometry reflects smoothly sanded hardwood corners, bevel-cut premium blueboard, and brass joinery. Corners are distinct and friendly, avoiding cold needle points while steering clear of overly toy-like full pills.

### Geometry Values
- **Default Radius (`rounded`):** 0.5rem (8px) for buttons, inputs, filter toggles, and metadata tags.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`):** 1rem (16px) for puzzle catalog cards, dialog panels, image framing, and cart drawers.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`rounded-xl`):** 1.5rem (24px) for hero containers, feature vignettes, and promotional banner modules.
- **Icon Enclosures:** Micro circles (`rounded-full`) are permissible strictly for avatar chips, color-way swatches, and bookmark badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#C25E2E`, text `#FDFBF7`, `rounded` (8px), padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle tactile depth with a bottom border-edge tone (`#A84D23`). Hover: shifts to `#B35225` with warm diffused elevation.
- **Secondary / Craft Outline:** Background `#F7F3EB`, text `#292524`, border `1px solid #C5BBA8`. Hover: background `#EDE5D8`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Text `#C25E2E`, underline offset 4px, hover color `#78350F`.

### Product Cards
- Container background `#F7F3EB`, 1px border `#E6DEC9`, radius `rounded-lg` (16px).
- Image wrapper with 4:5 aspect ratio, recessed with an inner border to resemble custom picture framing.
- Content stack: difficulty rating (sage dots), piece count (`label-sm`), serif title (`headline-sm`), and terracotta price display.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: 32px; font: `label-md`.
- Unselected: Surface `#F7F3EB`, border `1px solid #E6DEC9`, text `#78716C`.
- Selected: Surface `#EDE5D8`, border `1.5px solid #4A6B53`, text `#4A6B53`, accompanied by a check or subtle botanical indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Background `#FDFBF7`, 1px border `#D6CEB9`, radius `rounded` (8px). Focus state: border `#C25E2E` with an ambient glow ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(194, 94, 46, 0.15)`.
- Checkboxes: Custom square tiles with softened 3px corners, terracotta fill on active check, displaying a clean hand-pressed tick icon.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Difficulty Meter:** A linear gauge using 5 segmented puzzle peg silhouettes (1–5) colored in `#4A6B53` (Moss Sage).
- **Material Specs Ribbon:** Micro-badging row showcasing icons for "Sustainably Harvested Basswood", "Laser-Cut Precision", and "Matte Glare-Free Finish".
- **Mystery Archive Seal:** Circular wax-seal badge using `#78350F` and `#D97706` metallic accents to designate serialized, limited-run puzzle boxes.
